Frequently Asked Questions

We work with trusted local suppliers and cold-chain partners to keep fruits, vegetables and chilled items at the right temperature. Your order is packed carefully and delivered within the selected time slot so everything arrives fresh and ready to use.

Ecobazar currently delivers to selected areas in your city. Delivery slots are shown at checkout based on your address. You’ll see available same-day or next-day slots before you confirm your order.

You can pay securely using credit/debit cards and supported digital wallets. All payments are processed over encrypted connections to keep your data safe.

If something is missing, damaged or not as fresh as expected, contact our support within 24 hours. We’ll review your order and arrange a replacement or a refund according to our refund policy.

You can browse products without an account, but you’ll need to sign up or log in to complete your purchase, track orders, save addresses and view your order history on Ecobazar.
