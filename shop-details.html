Ecobazar avocados are carefully selected for perfect ripeness, creamy texture and naturally rich flavour. Each piece is hand-checked to make sure the outer skin is firm yet yields slightly to gentle pressure, so you get the ideal balance between softness and freshness.

These avocados are perfect for toast, salads, smoothies, sushi rolls and homemade guacamole. Simply slice, scoop and serve – no extra preparation needed. They are a great source of healthy fats, fibre and vitamins that support a balanced everyday diet.

  • Naturally creamy texture with rich, buttery taste.
  • Packed with healthy fats, fibre, and vitamin E.
  • Ideal for breakfast bowls, sandwiches and salads.
  • Sourced from trusted farms and cold-chain handled.

For best results, store firm avocados at room temperature until they soften slightly. Once ripe, refrigerate and consume within 2–3 days. To prevent browning after cutting, sprinkle with lemon or lime juice and keep in an airtight container.
